### 🚀 Engineering Overview

Mobile Software Engineer with **3+ years** of production experience shipping high-performance cross-platform apps across **fintech, social commerce, health-tech, logistics, and enterprise ecosystems**. 

* **Track Record:** Delivered **13+ production applications**, with active live products on the **App Store** and **Google Play**.
